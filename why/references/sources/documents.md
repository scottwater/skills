# Long-form documents

## Evidence

Product requirements, technical specifications, decision records, design reviews, meeting notes, strategy documents, postmortems, and runbooks can record rationale and rejected options in full.

## Search

Search the available document systems for:

- feature and subsystem names;
- symbols, error strings, and user-facing terms;
- linked ticket or review IDs;
- authors and owning teams;
- dates around proposal, implementation, and launch.

Read complete candidate documents, including comments, revisions, child pages, appendices, and linked decisions when accessible. Identify whether each source was a draft, approved decision, or later retrospective.

## Strong evidence

A finalized decision record, specification, or postmortem explicitly names the context, options, decision, and consequences tied to the target.

## Pitfalls

- planned designs may diverge from implementation;
- several drafts may contain superseded decisions;
- required rationale sections may be boilerplate;
- restricted pages and retention limits create gaps;
- later documents may explain the current reason rather than the original one.

## Return

Give title, stable link, authors, dates, status, decisive quotation and section, plus any contradiction with the shipped code or another document. Record search terms and inaccessible candidates.

**Complete when:** every candidate whose title or preview matches the target has been read or marked inaccessible, and linked decision or alternatives sections have been followed.
