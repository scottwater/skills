# Real-time team discussion

## Evidence

Team chat can preserve incident deliberation, design trade-offs, review questions, small decisions that never reached a document, and post-launch corrections.

## Search

1. Search linked review and ticket URLs or IDs.
2. Search feature names, symbols, error text, and informal synonyms.
3. Bound searches around the target's author, owning team, and ship window.
4. Search likely project, engineering, review, and incident channels when channel discovery is available.
5. Fetch complete threads and nearby context for each relevant message.

Inspect the connector's supported scope and authentication before searching. Record whether direct messages, private channels, archived channels, or older messages are excluded.

## Strong evidence

A complete thread contains an attributed statement of the decision or constraint and ties it to the same change, incident, or ticket.

## Pitfalls

- retention can erase the relevant window;
- direct or private conversations may be outside search scope;
- jokes and brainstorming are weaker than recorded decisions;
- an isolated message can reverse meaning when removed from its thread;
- author identity and timestamps may be ambiguous across exports.

## Return

Provide channel or conversation, permalink or stable identifier, participants, date range, exact quotations, and thread context. Record queries, scope exclusions, and retention gaps.

**Complete when:** linked IDs, target terms, and author-bounded searches have been run across the accessible window, and every relevant thread has been read in full.
