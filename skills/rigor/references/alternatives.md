# Explore consequential alternatives

- Compare two or three materially different designs.
- Use a throwaway prototype when observation can answer the question more cheaply than debate.
- Judge alternatives by the stated user outcome, system constraints, maintainability, and evidence. Treat implementation convenience as one cost rather than the product goal.

**Complete when:** every viable alternative has faced the same decision criteria and the selected design names the evidence or tradeoff that decided it.
